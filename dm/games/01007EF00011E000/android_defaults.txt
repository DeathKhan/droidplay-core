# Switches this game gets on Android (build_android_from_nsp.py puts them in the game's defaults.txt; env.txt and the settings screen still win)
# DM_VK_MID_DRAWS: a chunk goes to the GPU after 512 draws in the middle of a pass instead of 64 (gfx_vk.c mid_poll). About 3,600 draws a frame in long passes: on a
# OnePlus Pad 3 the replay took 30.6 ms of CPU a frame instead of 32.2 (fewer submissions) and the GPU 18.7 ms instead of 21.6 (fewer passes begun again with their targets
# loaded), the Shrine of Resurrection save, 2026-11-10. Measured with descriptor set reuse on and one draw skipped by a local test switch: on main that draw hangs the
# Pad 3's GPU and the device is lost at the first frame in the field (dm/android/baselines.md problem 14)
DM_VK_MID_DRAWS=512
# DM_VK_SET_REUSE: constant buffers as dynamic uniform buffers and descriptor sets bound again within a chunk (gfx_vk_draw.inc sr_find): 87% of its sets not written, the replay
# 1.3 ms faster. DM_VK_RECORDER: the replay's vkCmd* calls and submissions on a thread of their own (gfx_vk_rec.inc). DM_FPS_TARGET=30: the frame step holds 30 (2 vsyncs) as
# its floor, the Switch's own rate. Together on a OnePlus Pad 3 (the Shrine of Resurrection save, 2026-11-10): 30.0 fps, frame p95 38.1-38.9 ms, the replay 17.2-17.5 ms of CPU
# a frame; with the 30 target alone 29.2-29.6 fps (frame p95 41-44 ms); with the recorder alone at the 60 target it stayed at 20 fps in four runs of five (the clocks stay low at
# hold 3). Measured on the Pad 3 with one draw skipped by a local test switch: on main that draw hangs the Pad 3's GPU and the device is lost at the first frame in the field
# (dm/android/baselines.md problem 14), so BotW does not run there without it. On an Ayn Odin 2 (Adreno 740) that draw runs, and nothing is skipped (same save, 2026-11-10,
# frames 2200-3350, past the end of loading): 30.0 fps with no frame over 40 ms (frame p95 35.9-36.3 ms), the replay 16.7-16.9 ms; without these three, 30.3-30.8 fps with
# 16-19 frames over 40 ms (frame p95 37.6-38.4 ms), the replay 24.9-25.1 ms, as the frame step tries 60 and fails; set reuse and the recorder at the 60 target, about 190 frames
# in 1,150 at 60 among the 30s (the step tries 60 ten times a run and fails)
DM_VK_SET_REUSE=1
DM_VK_RECORDER=1
DM_FPS_TARGET=30
